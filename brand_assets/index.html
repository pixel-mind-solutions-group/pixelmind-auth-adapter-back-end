Brand Integrity Guidelines

Rules to maintain consistency, credibility, and brand recognition across all touchpoints worldwide.

Always Do
  • Always spell the brand name with exact capitalization: meskOra (lowercase "m", "esk", capital "O", lowercase "ra").
  • Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least 0.5× the height of the symbol mark.
  • Use the official vector SVGs from the brand_assets/svg/ directory to ensure crisp rendering at all DPIs.
  • Use the dark background version on slate, navy, or black interfaces to preserve luminosity and contrast.
  • Use the compact horizontal version whenever vertical height is restricted below 48px.
Never Do
  • Never alter the capitalization to "Meskora", "MESKORA", or "meskora" in primary logotypes.
  • Never stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the geometric proportions of the 'mO' symbol mark.
  • Never add drop shadows, outer glows, or bevels beyond the approved digital lighting guidelines.
  • Never place the full-color logo over busy, high-contrast photographic backgrounds without a dark tinted overlay.
  • Never swap colors with unapproved shades (e.g. green, orange, or red) outside seasonal marketing badges.
